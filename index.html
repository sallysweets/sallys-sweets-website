BAKING BASICS

Little details, better bakes.

Brown butter is a lifestyle.

Honestly, taking that extra 5 minutes to brown your butter is a total game changer. It makes everything taste incredibly rich and nutty.

Room temp is not a suggestion.

I used to skip this when I was rushing, but seriously, cold eggs and butter will completely ruin your dough's texture. Let them chill on the counter first!

Trust the timer, but use your eyes.

Every oven is completely different! Start checking a few minutes early, and let the golden edges be your final answer.

MEET THE BAKER

About Sally

Hi there! I’m Sally Al Khayyat. I’m 20 years old, originally born in Baghdad, Iraq, and I'm currently a junior majoring in Biomedical Sciences at Arizona State University, working towards my dream of becoming an Anesthesiologist!

My baking journey actually started out of pure boredom during winter break of my sophomore year in high school. Armed with nothing but determination and countless YouTube videos, I taught myself completely from scratch. Once I finally perfected French macarons, I started selling them, and just like that, Sally's Sweets was officially born!

Since those early days, I've pushed myself to expand my menu, learning how to make from-scratch cookies, rich brownies, tiramisu, and more. Everything I make is 100% self-taught and baked with passion.
