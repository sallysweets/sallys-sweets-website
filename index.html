About Sally

Hi there! I’m Sally. I’m 20 years old, born in Baghdad, Iraq, and moved to the United States when I was 7 years old. I’m currently a junior majoring in Biomedical Sciences with aspirations of becoming an Anesthesiologist! Outside the kitchen and lecture halls, I love playing soccer whenever I get the chance. ⚽✨

My baking journey started during winter break of my sophomore year in high school. I decided to challenge myself by creating my own business—Sally’s Sweets.
