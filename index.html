MEET THE BAKER

About Sally

Hi there! I’m Sally. I’m 20 years old, originally born in Baghdad, Iraq, and I'm currently a junior majoring in Biomedical Sciences at Arizona State University, working towards my dream of becoming an Anesthesiologist! Outside of the kitchen and my pre-med classes, my absolute biggest obsession is soccer. Honestly, if I didn't have to study or work, I would drop everything to be a pro soccer player and just live on the field! ⚽✨

My baking journey actually started out of pure boredom during winter break of my sophomore year in high school. I wanted a challenge, so I decided to tackle one of the notoriously hardest things you can bake: French macarons. Armed with nothing but determination and countless YouTube videos, I taught myself completely from scratch. Once I finally perfected them, I made my own business cards and started selling them to my teachers and classmates. They absolutely loved them, and just like that, Sally's Sweets was officially born!

Since those early macaron days, I've continuously pushed myself to expand my menu, learning how to make from-scratch cookies, rich brownies, tiramisu, custom cakes, and even baked Alaska. Everything I make is 100% self-taught, heavily researched, and baked with a whole lot of passion.
